# 14 — Content Plan

Content is the mechanism behind the client's stated goal. A technically perfect
site with thin copy will not be cited by an assistant. This plan says what to
write, in what order, and how to write it so it gets quoted.

---

## 1. How to write for AI citation

Six rules. They apply to every page and every article.

1. **Answer in the first sentence.** A section titled "Can a VA buyer purchase
   new construction in Florida?" opens with "Yes — VA loans can be used for new
   construction, but the builder must be VA-registered and the home must pass a
   VA appraisal." Then explain. Assistants extract the first clear statement.
2. **Use question-shaped headings.** Real questions people type, not marketing
   labels.
3. **Be specific and local.** Name cities, ZIP codes, counties, school
   districts, builders, communities, roads. "Central Florida" is unquotable;
   "Lake Mary, Seminole County, ZIP 32746, Seminole County Public Schools" is.
4. **Date every number.** Every statistic carries an "as of" date. An undated
   median price becomes wrong within a quarter and damages trust.
5. **Write in the first person, from experience.** "In 13 years working Lake
   Mary, the mistake I see most often is…" is an experience signal that generic
   content cannot fake. She is the source; write as her.
6. **Use tables and lists for facts.** Prose for reasoning, structured markup
   for data. Structured data is easier to extract and easier to cite.

Never write filler to hit a word count. A tight 900-word page that actually
answers something outperforms 2,500 words of padding.

## 7. Content style guide

- **Voice:** confident, plain, specific. She is an expert who explains things
  clearly, not a salesperson.
- **Person:** first person for her expertise ("I check…"), second person for the
  reader ("you will need…").
- **Sentence length:** vary. Break any sentence over 25 words.
- **Jargon:** define on first use. "Entitlement" and "MPR" are not common
  knowledge.
- **Numbers:** always dated, always sourced.
- **Claims:** never guarantee a price, a timeline or an outcome.
- **Fair Housing:** describe the property, never the ideal occupant. The full
  prohibited-language list is in `09-compliance-legal.md` § 2 and is enforced by
  a linter in the admin editor.
- **Formatting:** short paragraphs (2–4 sentences), H2 every 200–300 words,
  tables for comparisons, bulleted lists for anything enumerable.
- **Links:** descriptive anchors; every guide links to relevant cities and
  listings; every city links to its guides.
- **Images:** every one earns its place and has real alt text.
- **CTA:** one clear next action per page, repeated at most twice.
